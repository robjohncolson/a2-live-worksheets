How your grade works

Assessments 50% · Assignments 40% · Engagement 10%.

Quizzes and topic assessments earn Assessments points. Try-Its earn Assignments points. Flashcards count through daily Engagement redemption.

Unattempted due work counts as zero after the lesson day, except work due through Sep 18: those bonus items count only when attempted and only when they help the category grade. The live grade includes it; Schoology today includes due, completed work using the same district formula.

The ceiling is the best grade still reachable from remaining work.
