Algebra 2 — Period G

Lynn English High School · 2026–27 · Meets Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday. Early-release Wednesdays are still class days.

The course

We use enVision Algebra 2. The year covers functions and their graphs, quadratics, polynomials, and rational functions, then bridges into radicals, exponentials, and trigonometry. Bring a charged Chromebook, a notebook, and a pencil every day.

An average day

Sit down and open the Desk on your Chromebook; no phones. I work the lesson's Examples in OneNote on the board while you copy them into your notes. The notebook link is on Schoology, so if you are absent, use it to catch up on what we worked through. Then you work the Try-Its while I walk around and score each one 0, 1, or 2 on my phone. You take the short lesson check on your Chromebook. If there is time, run the lesson's flashcard deck to practice the rules and vocabulary. Check the Desk for your recorded scores before you pack up and leave.

I use the district formula: Assessments 50%, Assignments 40%, Engagement 10%. Within each category, I divide points earned by points possible for work that is due. A 100-point topic assessment has more weight than a 10-point lesson check. Unattempted due work counts as zero after the lesson day ends, with the add/drop exception below.

The minimums per quarter are 4 Assessments, 10 Assignments, and 10 Engagement items. The Desk shows your progress toward those counts alongside your live grade. If a category has no counted work yet, the grade uses the categories that do. The ceiling shows what is still reachable by finishing remaining work perfectly; it does not predict future retake scores. Schoology matches the Desk's district formula.

Bonus

Schoology has one Bonus column in Assignments. It is out of 0 points, so it can only add to your grade. You can earn up to 10 bonus points each quarter. Your best work from the first two weeks started it, with up to 3 points. You add to it with IXL homework finished at 80 or higher and with Blooket games, scored on questions answered and accuracy. Blooket counts only as bonus and never against you.

The first two weeks

Add/drop is open through Friday, Sep 18. Work due on or before Sep 18 can only raise your grade: I count it only when it helps, and I never count it against you. After Sep 18, due work you have not attempted counts as zero.
