    Quarter Close & Bonus

    At the end of a quarter, Freeze it — this snapshots every student's current quarter grade as the authoritative closed-quarter record (those are the numbers you enter into Schoology). Freezing is one-way per quarter: the FIRST freeze wins. Afterward, Bonus deltas lists anyone whose grade rose since the freeze (e.g. a makeup PC), so you can grant the difference as extra credit in the new quarter. Does not change how any grade computes — it only records + compares.
